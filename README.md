## ✨ Features

StoryForge is designed to be the ultimate distraction-free writing environment for novelists, packed with tools to help you organize your world and stay inspired.

### 📚 Visual Bookshelf & Organization
*   **Interactive Home Screen:** Manage your novels on a beautiful, visual wooden bookshelf.
*   **Project Dashboard:** Keep track of your book's summary, total word count, and all your writing modules in one place.

### ✍️ Distraction-Free Writing
*   **Zen Mode:** Hide all UI elements to focus purely on your manuscript.
*   **Smart Editor:** Features auto-save, live word/character count, and a comfortable serif font.
*   **Export to Text:** Instantly compile and download your entire novel as a formatted `.txt` file.

### 🌍 World-Building Modules
*   **Character Profiles:** Create detailed profiles including role, appearance, personality, and backstory.
*   **Settings & Scenery:** Document locations with sensory details (sight, sound, smell) for consistent world-building.
*   **Plot Outliner:** Structure your story by assigning plot points to specific Acts (Beginning, Middle, Climax, Ending).

### 🎧 Spotify-Style Music Player
*   **Built-in Audio Library:** Upload and store your own MP3 files directly in the app.
*   **Custom Player:** Enjoy a sleek, dark-themed music player with play/pause, skip, and seek controls.
*   **Background Playback:** Keep listening to your writing playlist even while in Zen Mode.

### 🐱 Interactive Companion
*   **Customizable Cat:** Choose from different animated GIFs, adjust size, and control walking speed.
*   **Fully Interactive:** Tap to pet (spawns hearts!), drag to move, and double-tap to freeze your companion.

### 🔍 Smart Tools
*   **Global Search:** Instantly find specific characters, locations, or keywords across all your chapters and notes.
*   **Writing Assistant:** Cure writer's block with random prompts and stay motivated with daily word goals.

### 📱 Progressive Web App (PWA)
*   **Installable:** Add directly to your mobile home screen for a native app experience.
*   **Offline Ready:** Works entirely without an internet connection once loaded.
*   **Unlimited Local Storage:** Powered by IndexedDB, allowing you to store massive manuscripts and audio files directly on your device.
